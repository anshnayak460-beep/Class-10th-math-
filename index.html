📘 Lesson 1 — Real Numbers

⭐ Important Concepts

Euclid's Division Lemma:
a = bq + r, where 0 ≤ r < b
Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic:
Every composite number can be expressed as a product of primes, and this factorisation is unique apart from the order of the factors.
For two positive integers:
HCF × LCM = Product of the two numbers

Q1. What is Euclid's Division Lemma?

Answer: For positive integers a and b, there exist unique integers q and r such that:

a = bq + r, where 0 ≤ r < b.

Q2. Find the HCF of 135 and 225 using Euclid's algorithm.

Answer:
225 = 135 × 1 + 90
135 = 90 × 1 + 45
90 = 45 × 2 + 0

Therefore, HCF = 45.

Q3. Find the HCF of 96 and 404.

Answer:
404 = 96 × 4 + 20
96 = 20 × 4 + 16
20 = 16 × 1 + 4
16 = 4 × 4 + 0

Therefore, HCF = 4.

Q4. Find the prime factorisation of 140.

Answer:
140 = 2 × 70
= 2 × 2 × 35
= 2² × 5 × 7

Therefore, 140 = 2² × 5 × 7.

Q5. Find the HCF and LCM of 12 and 18.

Answer:
12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²

HCF = 2 × 3 = 6
LCM = 2² × 3² = 36

Q6. Is √2 a rational number?

Answer: No. √2 is an irrational number. It cannot be expressed in the form p/q where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0.

Q7. What is a rational number?

Answer: A number that can be written in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0, is called a rational number.

Q8. What is an irrational number?

Answer: A number that cannot be expressed in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0, is called an irrational number. Examples: √2, √3, √5.
